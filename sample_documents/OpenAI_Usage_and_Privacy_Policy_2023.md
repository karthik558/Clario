# OpenAI Usage and Privacy Policy (2023)
**Document Identifier:** OAI-POL-2023-REV3  
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2023  
**Governing Jurisdiction:** State of California, United States  
**Scope:** Developer API Services, ChatGPT Consumer Services, and Commercial Integrations  

---

### Section 1: Data Retention & Logging Protocols
OpenAI maintains a default data retention window for API interactions:
1. **API Customer Retention:** Customer inputs and generated completions submitted via the commercial API are retained for a maximum of **30 days** solely for abuse monitoring and security investigations.
2. **Permanent Deletion:** After the 30-day monitoring window expires, API logs are permanently purged from production systems, unless legally required otherwise.
3. **Model Training:** OpenAI does **not** use customer data submitted via the commercial API to train or fine-tune our foundation models.
4. **Consumer ChatGPT Data:** Interactions through non-API consumer ChatGPT are retained and may be used for model training unless users explicitly opt-out in their account privacy settings.

### Section 2: Universal Usage Policies and Disallowed Applications
Developers and organizations accessing OpenAI models may not build or facilitate:
- **Weapons & Military Combat:** Development of kinetic military weapons, chemical munitions, or automated tactical targeting systems.
- **High-Risk Autonomous Decision Making:** Fully autonomous eligibility determination for credit, housing, or criminal sentencing without human intervention.
- **Deceptive Content & Non-Consensual Imagery:** Generation of non-consensual sexual content, deepfakes, or impersonation of individuals without consent.
- **Financial Market Manipulation:** Automated dissemination of deceptive high-frequency financial misinformation.

### Section 3: Commercial Liability & Financial Limitations
To the maximum extent permitted by applicable California law, OpenAI's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to developer API services shall not exceed the greater of **$100** or the total fees paid by the customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to liability. OpenAI shall not be liable for indirect, punitive, or consequential damages.

### Section 4: Notice of Breach and Termination
Either party may terminate this agreement upon providing at least **30 days** formal written notice detailing material breach, during which the defaulting party may attempt to cure the identified non-compliance.
