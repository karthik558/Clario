# Master Commercial Cloud Service Agreement
**Contract Number:** NX-AGR-2023-884  
**Date of Execution:** May 10, 2023  
**Parties:** NexusAI Platform Technologies Inc. ("Provider") and Enterprise Licensee ("Customer")  

---

### Clause 1: Services and Scope of License
Provider grants to Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable worldwide license to access the NexusAI semantic parsing and natural language inference APIs during the Subscription Term.

### Clause 2: Limitation of Liability
To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, in no event shall Provider's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement exceed the lesser of $50,000 or the total fees paid by Customer in the three (3) months preceding the incident giving rise to liability. Under no circumstances shall Provider be liable for lost profits, loss of data, or indirect, consequential, or punitive damages.

### Clause 3: Termination and Notice of Breach
Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach upon providing at least 30 days written notice specifying the breach, provided the breaching party has failed to cure such defect within the 30-day cure window.

### Clause 4: Intellectual Property and Customer Data
Customer retains all title and ownership rights in datasets uploaded to the platform. Customer grants Provider an irrevocable, royalty-free license to utilize anonymized customer query embeddings for platform performance benchmarking and system telemetry improvements.

### Clause 5: Indemnification Obligations
Customer agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Provider from and against any third-party claims, liabilities, damages, and legal costs arising from customer data inputs or violations of applicable export control regulations.
