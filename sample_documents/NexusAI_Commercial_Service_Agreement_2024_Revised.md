# Master Commercial Cloud Service Agreement (Revised)
**Contract Number:** NX-AGR-2024-912R  
**Date of Execution:** June 1, 2024  
**Parties:** NexusAI Platform Technologies Inc. ("Provider") and Enterprise Licensee ("Customer")  

---

### Clause 1: Services and Scope of License
Provider grants to Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable worldwide license to access the NexusAI enterprise neural retrieval and document intelligence APIs during the Subscription Term.

### Clause 2: Limitation of Liability
To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, the total aggregate liability of either party arising under or related to this Agreement shall be capped at $250,000 or the total fees paid by Customer during the twelve (12) months preceding the claim, whichever is greater. Neither party shall be liable for consequential damages unless caused by gross negligence or willful misconduct.

### Clause 3: Termination and Notice of Breach
Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately for material breach if the defaulting party fails to remedy the breach within 60 days following formal delivery of written notice detailing the breach.

### Clause 4: Intellectual Property and Customer Data
Customer retains sole and exclusive ownership of all customer data, documents, and derived embeddings. Provider shall strictly not utilize customer text, queries, or derived vector representations to train, fine-tune, or benchmark any external or shared artificial intelligence models.

### Clause 5: Mutual Indemnification Obligations
Each party agrees to mutually defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the other party against third-party claims alleging direct infringement of intellectual property rights, subject to prompt written notification of such action.
